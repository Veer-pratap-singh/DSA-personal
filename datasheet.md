# GATE Data Science & Artificial Intelligence (DA) Topper-Approved 6-Month Study Datasheet

This datasheet provides a comprehensive, day-by-day guide, free resource list, and strategy roadmap to crack the GATE Data Science & AI (DA) paper in 6 months (180 days).

---

## 1. Exam Pattern & Marks Distribution
The GATE DA paper consists of two main sections:
*   **General Aptitude (GA):** 15 Marks (10 questions: 5 of 1-mark and 5 of 2-marks).
*   **Core Technical Subjects:** 85 Marks (55 questions: 25 of 1-mark and 30 of 2-marks).
*   **Question Types:** 
    *   **MCQs** (Multiple Choice Questions - negative marking applies)
    *   **MSQs** (Multiple Select Questions - NO negative marking, no partial marking)
    *   **NATs** (Numerical Answer Type - NO negative marking)

---

## 2. Topic Weightage Analysis
Based on trends and syllabus structure, prioritize subjects in this order:
1.  **Probability & Statistics:** High weightage (~15-18%). Core foundation for ML.
2.  **Machine Learning:** High weightage (~15-18%). Directly tests conceptual and mathematical understanding.
3.  **Linear Algebra:** Medium-High weightage (~12-15%). Essential for understanding PCA, SVD, and ML models.
4.  **Database Management Systems (DBMS):** Medium weightage (~10-12%). Very scoring.
5.  **Programming, Data Structures & Algorithms:** Medium weightage (~10-12%). Focus on Python, complexity, and basic structures.
6.  **Artificial Intelligence:** Medium weightage (~8-10%). Search and Logic.
7.  **Calculus & Optimization:** Lower-Medium weightage (~6-8%). Optimization (Gradient Descent) is crucial for ML.

## 4. 180-Day (6-Month) Study Plan

### Phase 1: Mathematical Foundations (Days 1 - 60)
*Assumed Hours: 5-6 hours/day. Focus on derivation, mathematical proofs, and building strong intuition.*

#### Week 1: Matrices & Systems of Linear Equations
*   **Day 1-2:** Vectors, linear combinations, span, linear independence, and dependence.
*   **Day 3-4:** Systems of linear equations ($Ax = b$), Gaussian elimination, row reduction, and Row Echelon Form.
*   **Day 5-6:** Rank of a matrix, Nullity, and the Rank-Nullity Theorem.
*   **Day 7:** Review Week 1 topics, write short summary formulas, rate your understanding.

#### Week 2: Vector Spaces & Fundamental Subspaces
*   **Day 8-9:** Vector spaces and subspaces. Examples, properties, and verification.
*   **Day 10-11:** Bases and dimension of a vector space. Change of basis.
*   **Day 12-13:** The Four Fundamental Subspaces: Column Space $C(A)$, Nullspace $N(A)$, Row Space $C(A^T)$, Left Nullspace $N(A^T)$.
*   **Day 14:** Solve practice questions on bases and dimension. Complete weekly self-analysis.

#### Week 3: Linear Transformations & Eigenvalues
*   **Day 15-16:** Matrix multiplication as a transformation, Kernel, Range, and Inverse of matrices.
*   **Day 17-18:** Determinants: properties, Cramer's rule, geometrical meaning.
*   **Day 19-20:** Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors, Characteristic Equation, Diagonalization, Similarity transformation.
*   **Day 21:** Solve previous GATE questions (CS/EC/EE math papers) on Eigenvalues/Eigenvectors.

#### Week 4: Orthogonality & Decompositions (Crucial for ML)
*   **Day 22-23:** Inner product, length, orthogonality, orthogonal projections.
*   **Day 24-25:** Gram-Schmidt Orthogonalization process, QR Decomposition.
*   **Day 26-27:** LU Decomposition and Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) concept.
*   **Day 28:** Linear Algebra subject test, analyze errors, document weak spots.

#### Week 5: Probability Basics & Bayes' Theorem
*   **Day 29-30:** Basic concepts: Sample space, events, axioms, counting principles.
*   **Day 31-32:** Conditional probability, independence, multiplication rule.
*   **Day 33-34:** Bayes' Theorem and Law of Total Probability. (Master this, it guarantees exam questions!)
*   **Day 35:** Practice Bayes' Theorem problems from GATE CS papers.

#### Week 6: Random Variables & Distributions
*   **Day 36-37:** Discrete Random Variables: PMF, CDF, Expectation, Variance, Moments.
*   **Day 38-39:** Continuous Random Variables: PDF, CDF, Expectation, Variance.
*   **Day 40-41:** Standard Distributions: Binomial, Poisson, Geometric, Uniform, Exponential, Normal. Learn their PMF/PDF, mean, and variance formulas by heart.
*   **Day 42:** Practice standard distribution problems. Write cheatsheets.

#### Week 7: Joint Distributions & Limit Theorems
*   **Day 43-44:** Joint distributions (discrete & continuous), marginal distributions, conditional distributions.
*   **Day 45-46:** Covariance, Correlation, Independence of multiple random variables.
*   **Day 47-48:** Chebyshev's Inequality, Law of Large Numbers (LLN), Central Limit Theorem (CLT).
*   **Day 49:** Weekly review and joint distributions practice test.

#### Week 8: Statistical Inference (Very High Weightage)
*   **Day 50-51:** Population vs. sample, sample mean, sample variance, t-distribution, Chi-square distribution.
*   **Day 52-53:** Estimation: Point estimation, Bias, MSE, Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE).
*   **Day 54-55:** Hypothesis Testing: Null/Alternative hypotheses, Type-I & Type-II errors, p-values, z-test, t-test, chi-square test.
*   **Day 56:** Complete Statistics subject test, log score and mistakes.

#### Week 9: Single-Variable Calculus
*   **Day 57-58:** Limits, continuity, differentiability of single-variable functions.
*   **Day 59-60:** Mean Value Theorems (Rolle's, Lagrange's, Cauchy's). Taylor Series expansions.
*   **Day 61-62:** Maxima and minima of single-variable functions.
*   **Day 63:** Weekly review & practice.

#### Week 10: Multivariable Calculus & Optimization
*   **Day 64-65:** Functions of multiple variables, partial derivatives, Gradient vector, Hessian Matrix.
*   **Day 66-67:** Maxima, minima, saddle points of multivariable functions.
*   **Day 68-69:** Unconstrained optimization basics, Gradient Descent algorithm (learning rate, update step).
*   **Day 70:** Calculus & Optimization subject test.

---

### Phase 2: Programming, DSA & DBMS (Days 71 - 98)
*Assumed Hours: 5-6 hours/day. Focus on logic, code trace, queries, and normalization algorithms.*

#### Week 11: Python Basics & Complexity Analysis
*   **Day 71-72:** Python syntax, lists, tuples, dicts, strings, loops, functions, file I/O, lambda functions.
*   **Day 73-74:** Complexity analysis: Asymptotic notations (Big O, $\Omega$, $\Theta$). Recursion and recurrence relations.
*   **Day 75-76:** Arrays, Linked Lists, Stacks, Queues (operations, complexities, basic implementations).
*   **Day 77:** Review and practice coding questions in Python.

#### Week 12: Trees, Graphs, Sorting & Searching
*   **Day 78-79:** Binary Trees, Binary Search Trees (BST) - insertions, deletions, traversals. Heaps (Max/Min) and heap operations.
*   **Day 80-81:** Graphs: representation (matrix, list), BFS, DFS traversals.
*   **Day 82-83:** Searching (Linear, Binary) and Sorting (Bubble, Insertion, Merge, Quick, Heap sort) with their time and space complexities.
*   **Day 84:** DSA subject test and review.

#### Week 13: ER Model & Relational Database Management
*   **Day 85-86:** ER model: entities, attributes, relationships, weak entities, cardinality.
*   **Day 87-88:** Relational Model: Relational algebra operations, candidate/primary/foreign keys, integrity constraints.
*   **Day 89-90:** SQL: queries, joins, nested queries, subqueries, group by, having, aggregate functions.
*   **Day 91:** SQL writing practice (Solve queries on platforms like LeetCode or GeeksforGeeks).

#### Week 14: Normalization, Transactions & Warehousing
*   **Day 92-93:** Functional Dependencies, Attribute closure, Normalization: 1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF. Lossless join & dependency preservation checks.
*   **Day 94-95:** Transactions: ACID properties, Serializability (Conflict & View), Concurrency Control.
*   **Day 96-97:** Indexing: B-trees and B+ trees structures (node capacities, search path). Data Warehousing: Star and Snowflake schemas, basic OLAP.
*   **Day 98:** DBMS subject test and review.

---

### Phase 3: Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence (Days 99 - 140)
*Assumed Hours: 6-7 hours/day. Focus on both mathematics behind the models and operational mechanisms.*

#### Week 15: Introduction & Regression Models
*   **Day 99-100:** Introduction to ML: Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement. Underfitting, Overfitting, Bias-Variance Tradeoff, Cross-Validation.
*   **Day 101-102:** Simple and Multiple Linear Regression. Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) derivation, cost function, assumptions.
*   **Day 103-104:** Regularization: Ridge (L2 penalty) and Lasso (L1 penalty, feature selection), Elastic Net.
*   **Day 105:** Review regression math. Practice question solving.

#### Week 16: Basic Classification Models
*   **Day 106-107:** Logistic Regression: Sigmoid function, cross-entropy loss, decision boundaries, odds ratio.
*   **Day 108-109:** Support Vector Machines (SVM): Hyperplanes, hard margin, soft margin (slack variables), Kernel Trick, Support Vectors.
*   **Day 110-111:** Decision Trees: Entropy, Gini Impurity, Information Gain, classification and regression trees, pruning.
*   **Day 112:** Solve practice problems on SVM boundaries and Information Gain.

#### Week 17: Naive Bayes, KNN & Neural Networks
*   **Day 113-114:** Naive Bayes classifier (conditional independence, smoothing) and K-Nearest Neighbors (distance metrics, boundary complexity).
*   **Day 115-117:** Neural Networks: Multi-Layer Perceptron, activation functions (Sigmoid, tanh, ReLU, Leaky ReLU, Softmax), forward propagation, backpropagation equations, loss functions (MSE, cross-entropy).
*   **Day 118:** Draw backpropagation computation graphs and solve small numerical steps.

#### Week 18: Unsupervised Learning & Dimensionality Reduction
*   **Day 119-120:** K-means clustering: objective function, convergence, elbow method, K-medoids.
*   **Day 121-122:** Hierarchical Clustering: Agglomerative vs. Divisive, Linkage criteria (Single, Complete, Average).
*   **Day 123-124:** Principal Component Analysis (PCA): Dimensionality reduction, covariance matrix, eigenvalues/eigenvectors, variance explained.
*   **Day 125:** Evaluation Metrics: Precision, Recall, F1-Score, ROC, AUC, Confusion Matrix.
*   **Day 126:** Machine Learning comprehensive subject test.

#### Week 19: Artificial Intelligence - Search Strategies
*   **Day 127-129:** Uninformed Search: BFS, DFS, Depth-Limited Search, Iterative Deepening. Completeness, optimality, time and space complexity.
*   **Day 130-132:** Informed Search: Greedy Best-First Search, A* Search (admissible & consistent heuristics). Adversarial Search: Minimax algorithm, Alpha-Beta Pruning.
*   **Day 133:** Solve game trees and calculate alpha-beta pruned branches.

#### Week 20: AI - Logic & Reasoning
*   **Day 134-135:** Propositional Logic: Syntax, semantics, truth tables, tautology, resolution refutation, soundness, and completeness.
*   **Day 136-137:** First-Order Logic (FOL): Quantifiers, unification, inference rules.
*   **Day 138-139:** Reasoning under uncertainty: Bayesian Networks, conditional independence, joint distribution factorizations.
*   **Day 140:** Artificial Intelligence subject test and review.

### Topper Tips for Revision:
1.  **The Feynman Technique:** Teach complex ML algorithms (like SVM kernel trick) to an imaginary person. If you get stuck, re-read the textbook.
2.  **Active Recall:** Instead of just re-reading notes, cover the notes and write down everything you remember about a topic (e.g., PCA steps) from scratch.
3.  **Spaced Repetition:** Revisit a topic after 3 days, then 7 days, then 30 days. This shifts the knowledge from short-term to long-term memory.
4.  **Mistake Book:** Whenever you get a question wrong in a test, write the question, the concept behind it, and the correct approach in a dedicated notebook. Read this notebook every Sunday.
